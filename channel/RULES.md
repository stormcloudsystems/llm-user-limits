# Show HN: rules (primary source, read 2026-10-20)

Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html. Nothing has been posted.

- For something people can try, ideally without signups or e-mails. This item runs locally with a bundled stub
  or a local Ollama model; no account or key.
- Must be non-trivial, "something you've worked on personally and which you're around to discuss"; explain how
  and why. Title begins with "Show HN". No landing pages, no asking others to upvote or comment.
- Owner decision before posting: the poster must have worked on it personally and be available to answer
  questions. It was built with AI coding agents; say so. Replace the [OWNER] placeholders; do not invent a story.
- Needs a public repository URL first (UNRESOLVED).
